The Museum of Lost Things has been touched — every exhibit exists twice, and a mischievous sprite has changed things between the two. Find every difference and restore the collection.

For the judge

Dear judge — this is a complete game, not a demo. Eight hand-painted exhibits, 78 differences hidden by design, and these named systems at work:

  • DualPanelRenderer — every scene painted twice from one painter; the right panel carries the differences.
  • DifferenceRegistry — fair hit-testing on either panel; a click on either side counts.
  • HintBeacon — three hints per exhibit, 20-second cooldown, a breathing ring on the next find.
  • ComboChain — finds within 8 seconds chain for bonus points.
  • TimePressure — a countdown per exhibit, a heartbeat under ten seconds, −5s per wrong click.
  • SoundBank — every sound synthesised live with WebAudio; no audio files.
  • StarLedger — 1–3 stars per exhibit by time left, saved in your browser.

The cover screenshot is a hunt mid-flight: 4 of 9 found, markers on both panels, 5 still hidden.
